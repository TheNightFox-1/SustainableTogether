# SustainableTogether Approach: Generic Sustainability Framework

Welcome to the **SustainableTogether Approach** documentation. This directory contains the holistic **8-layer sustainability transformation framework** and guidance for aligning all SustainableTogether project work.

---

## The Generic Approach Framework

The **Generic Approach** is a comprehensive sustainability transformation journey that guides how we evolve from a conventional enterprise (SolarX) into a sustainable one (SustainaSun).

### Framework Overview

![Generic Sustainability Approach Framework](SustainableTogether%20-%20Copy%20of%20Initial%20Generic%20Approach%20for%20the%20Case%20Study.jpg)

The framework consists of:
- **8 Strategic Layers** — the transformation journey from foundation to retirement
- **4 Holistic Topics** — cross-cutting disciplines that enable the entire transformation

---

## 8 Strategic Layers

### 1. Strategic & Conceptual Foundations
Where we define **why we transform** and what **principles guide us**.
- **Standards & Regulations** — EU ESPR, Digital Product Passport compliance
- **Mission & Vision** — shift from profit-maximization to eco-effectiveness
- **Design for Sustainable Behavior** — culture, incentives, and stakeholder engagement

*SustainableTogether focus:* Establish the vision for SustainaSun and understand regulatory landscape.

---

### 2. Product & Industrial Foundations
Where we design **what we offer** and **how we create value**.
- **Business Model** — transformation from product sale → PaaS (Product-as-a-Service) leasing
- **Enterprise Architecture** — how the organization delivers value (manufacturing-centric → service-centric)
- **Product Development** — design for longevity, repairability, and circular potential
- **Enabling Systems** — tools, processes, and infrastructure that support sustainable practices

*SustainableTogether focus:* Design SustainaSun's business model, service delivery architecture, and product evolution.

---

### 3. Industrialization & Production
Where we **scale sustainably** across manufacturing and supply chain.
- **Production** — manufacturing efficiency, resource optimization, emissions reduction
- **Supply Chain** — responsible sourcing, sustainable logistics, supplier engagement
- **Lifecycle Operation** — durability, maintenance systems, and lifecycle support

*SustainableTogether focus:* Map SolarX production baseline; design SustainaSun manufacturing and supply chain transformation.

---

### 4. Operation / Service
Where **customers realize value** and we optimize the use phase.
- **Product Use** — customer engagement, avoiding rebound effects, maximizing environmental benefit
- **R-Strategies** — the 9-level circular economy framework (Refuse → Recover)
- **Biological Cycle** — closed-loop material flows and regeneration

*SustainableTogether focus:* Design SustainaSun's service model and integrate circular economy strategies.

---

### 5. Retirement
Where we **eliminate waste** and **recover value**.
- **Goal:** Maximize material recovery, minimize landfill, enable second lives
- **Tools:** Digital Product Passport enables traceability for end-of-life routing

*SustainableTogether focus:* Design material reverse-logistics and circular recovery processes via DPP.

---

## 4 Holistic Topics (Cross-Cutting Disciplines)

These topics are **vertical integrators** that span all 8 layers and all 3 milestones:

### 1. Systems Thinking & System Dynamics
**Why:** Understand how sustainability factors interact within complex systems.
- Problem formulation and behavioral modeling
- Transformation dynamics and feedback loops
- Rebound effects and multi-scale impacts
- Contributes to all milestones via improved mental models

### 2. Digital Engineering (MBSE, Digital Twins, Simulation)
**Why:** Leverage model-based systems engineering and simulation to design and optimize for sustainability.
- MBSE models (SysML v2) as infrastructure for holistic view
- Digital twins for scenario analysis
- Simulation for optimization across layers
- Contributes to all milestones via integrated digital representation

### 3. Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)
**Why:** Quantify environmental impact across the full product lifecycle.
- ISO 14040/14044 standards and methodology
- Environmental baseline and comparative analysis
- Integration with SysML models for decision support
- M1: LCA baseline for all 5 SolarX components
- M2: Comparative LCA for SustainaSun scenarios
- M3: Environmental impact data for DPP

### 4. Risk Analysis
**Why:** Identify and manage sustainability-related risks across all dimensions.
- Environmental, social, regulatory, and supply chain risks
- Risk identification, assessment, and mitigation strategies
- Contributes to all milestones via proactive risk management

## How the Framework Maps to SustainableTogether Milestones

### Milestone 1: SolarX AS-IS Complete
**Objective:** Capture the current state of SolarX across all 8 layers.

- **Layers 1–5:** All represented in AS-IS baseline via SYSMOD (Steps 1–9)
- **LCA:** Baseline environmental impact for all 5 components (Issues #6–#8)
- **Digital Model:** SysML v2 MBSE as infrastructure
- **Outcome:** Complete reference model showing "where we are"

**Timeline:** Current focus (Issues #3–#9)

---

### Milestone 2: SustainaSun v1
**Objective:** Transform Layers 1–3 into a sustainable business model and enterprise architecture.

- **Layer 1 (Strategic):** Mission shift to eco-effectiveness and accessibility/affordability
- **Layer 2 (Product & Industrial):**
  - Business model transformation: product sale → PaaS + service delivery
  - Enterprise architecture: manufacturing → service-centric delivery
  - Product design for longevity and repairability
- **Layer 3 (Production):** Design sustainable manufacturing and supply chain
- **Layer 4 (Operation):** Design R-strategies as business differentiator
- **Layer 5 (Retirement):** Anticipate circular material recovery implications
- **LCA:** Comparative analysis of transformation scenarios
- **Outcome:** "TO-BE" model showing SustainaSun's sustainable operating model

**Timeline:** After Milestone 1 complete (Issues #10–#12)

---

### Milestone 3: DPP Integration
**Objective:** Close the loop via Digital Product Passport for full traceability.

- **Layers 1–5 integration:** DPP as infrastructure for transparency across all layers
  - Material composition (Layer 2–3)
  - Durability and maintenance data (Layer 4)
  - End-of-life routing (Layer 5)
  - ESPR regulatory compliance (Layer 1)
- **LCA:** Environmental impact data embedded in DPP
- **Outcome:** Integrated system enabling circular material recovery and regulatory compliance

**Timeline:** After Milestone 2 complete (Issues #13–#14)

## How to Use This Framework

### When Planning an Issue
1. **Identify which layers** your issue touches (e.g., "Product Design" = Layer 2)
2. **Check which holistic topics** are relevant (e.g., "Digital Engineering" for any model work)
3. **Confirm which milestone** it advances (M1, M2, or M3)
4. **Note dependencies** on other layers or milestones

### When Evaluating Progress
- Ask: "Does this advance Layer X in Milestone Y?"
- Map completed work back to the framework to track transformation progress
- Identify layer gaps that future workstreams should address

### When Coordinating WG Task Groups
- Use the layer/milestone map to identify which task groups should collaborate
- Ensure all 8 layers are represented across active workstreams
- Allocate leads to critical-path layers for each milestone

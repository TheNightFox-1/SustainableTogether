# Regenerative business model

A business model whose product or service offering is used to actively regenerate the natural
and societal spaces the firm operates in, creating value for all of them rather than
extracting from them [R03 p. 531, drawing on Hahn & Tampe 2021 and Konietzko et al. 2023].
R03 positions this as going beyond net zero to a net positive impact [R03 p. 529] — see
[net positive](net-positive.md).

## The six archetypes

R03's typology, derived from 84 evidenced cases across 15 sectors [R03 p. 529]:

1. regenerative leadership
2. nature regeneration
3. social regeneration
4. responsible sourcing
5. human health & wellbeing focus
6. employee level focus

Most firms in the database run several concurrently, usually with local partners [R03 p. 529].
The evidence base is documented separately as
[the Regenerative Business Case Database](../entities/regenerative-business-case-database.md).

## How a firm gets there

R05 addresses the transformation rather than the end state, using design science research to
build a three-step process template supported by transformative questions and six design
requirements. Its three phases are **re-grounding**, **re-wiring** and **re-seizing** the
business model [R05 abstract].

## The gap R04 identifies

R04's objection predates both and still stands: sustainability measures are feedback on
outputs, not design parameters. Assessment systems measure the effects of business-model
decisions; they cannot be used to formulate a coherent sustainable business model in the
first place [R04 introduction]. R03's typology and R05's process template are both attempts
at the design side of that gap.

## Contested

- **Whether the label survives assessment.** R02 places regenerative business models in its
  **weak fit** category for formal modelling [R02 MBSE addendum], and reports that regenerative
  claims frequently fail under system-boundary-corrected LCA [R02 gap analysis §3]. R03's
  inclusion criteria answer part of this by requiring evidenced, continuing activity and
  third-party verification where affordable [R03 p. 533], but verification of *claims* is not
  verification of *net effect*.
- **Unit of analysis.** This page uses the firm as the unit. [Regeneration](regeneration.md)
  records that R01's design tradition uses place instead. The two are not nested.

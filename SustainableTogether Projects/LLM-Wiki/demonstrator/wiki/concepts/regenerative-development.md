# Regenerative development

"A system of technologies and strategies for generating the patterned whole system
understanding of a place, and developing the strategic systemic thinking capacities, and the
stakeholder engagement/commitment required to ensure regenerative design processes to achieve
maximum systemic leverage and support, that is self-organizing and self-evolving"
[R01 glossary].

Read against [regenerative design](regenerative-design.md), the division is clean:
development builds the *understanding and the capacity to act*; design produces the
*intervention*. R01 makes place the carrier of that understanding — the "unique, multi-layered
network of ecosystems within a geographic region" — and locational patterns the means of
reading it [R01 glossary].

The stakeholder-capacity half of this definition is the part with no engineering analogue in
the corpus. It is also the part R02 classifies as resistant to formal modelling: story-of-place
methodology and regenerative leadership sit in its **weak fit** category, not because they are
unimportant but because their essential content is a relation rather than a flux
[R02 MBSE addendum].

# Restorative design

"Sometimes called restorative environmental design; a design system that combines returning
'polluted, degraded or damaged sites back to a state of acceptable health through human
intervention' with biophiliac designs that reconnect people to nature" [R01 glossary].

The operative words are *back to* and *acceptable health*. Restoration has a target state
drawn from the past and a stopping condition; regeneration, in R01's usage, has neither —
it aims at a system that keeps evolving [R01 glossary].

This is the sharpest usable boundary the corpus offers between the two terms, and it is worth
holding, because the WG will be asked for it.

## Contested

R02 does not hold the line. It treats "ecological restoration" and regeneration as
substantially the same field — the UN Decade on Ecosystem Restoration is entry 1.1 of a
taxonomy titled *Regeneration Across Earth's Systems* [R02 §1.1], and its synonym list for
that entry runs "Generation Restoration; ecological restoration". Against R01's distinction,
that is a category merge, not a synonym.

Both readings are defensible; they belong to different literatures. The WG has to pick one
for its own documents. Open as [Q02](../questions/q02-regeneration-scope.md).

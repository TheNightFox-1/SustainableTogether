# Tri-profit

R04's replacement for profit as the success metric of a strongly sustainable firm: "the
conceptual net sum of the costs (harms) and revenues (benefits) arising as a result of a
firm's activities in each of the environmental, social, and economic contexts in a given time
period, measured in units appropriate to each" [R04 FP4].

The load-bearing clause is *units appropriate to each*. R04 distinguishes tri-profit from
triple bottom line accounting explicitly: TBL calculates additional non-economic profits
using "a measurable common 'currency'", leaving the meaning of profit untouched and merely
applying it outside the economic field. Tri-profit re-conceptualises profit instead, and
refuses the common currency [R04 FP4, citing Norman & Macdonald 2004].

A tri-profitable firm creates sufficient financial rewards, social benefits and environmental
regeneration, with sufficiency defined by the stakeholders holding the governance rights to
define it [R04 FP4]. Sufficiency is therefore a governance output, not a calculation — which
is why the metric remains conceptual in R04's ontology rather than implemented as an
accounting practice.

## Contested

None in the corpus. But note the structural tension with [net positive](net-positive.md):
"net" invites a single figure, and tri-profit exists precisely to deny that a single figure
is available.

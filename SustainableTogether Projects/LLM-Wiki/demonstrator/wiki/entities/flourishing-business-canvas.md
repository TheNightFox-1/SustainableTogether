# Flourishing Business Canvas

A business-modelling canvas built on R04's strongly sustainable business model ontology,
documented for practitioners in R08 (v2.1 canvas, guide v3.0, 2023).

Two structural moves distinguish it from the profit-oriented business model canvas it
descends from. It sets the business model inside three nested contexts — environment, society
and economy — rather than treating them as externalities [R08 contents, §"Environment,
Society and Economy"]. And it reads any human enterprise through four perspectives: people,
value, process and outcome [R08 contents, §"The People, Value, Process and Outcome
Perspectives"].

The ontology underneath is R04's, whose relevant commitments are recorded on
[strong sustainability](../concepts/strong-sustainability.md) and [tri-profit](../concepts/tri-profit.md).

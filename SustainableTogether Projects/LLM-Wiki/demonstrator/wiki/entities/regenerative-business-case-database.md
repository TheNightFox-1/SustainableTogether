# Regenerative Business Case Database

R03's evidence base: 84 regenerative business cases across 15 sectors, built by purposive
sampling and published as Appendix S2 of the paper [R03 pp. 529, 533].

## How it was built

118 cases collected initially; 44 duplicates removed; 25 removed against the inclusion
criteria; 36 added on expert recommendation during writing — leaving 84 [R03 p. 533].

Inclusion required three things: the firm communicates the regenerative aspects of its
business model; the effort is continuing, with proof of concept rather than a stated
intention; and the firm generates active revenue. Independent third-party verification
(certification, audit) was required where available, with detailed public reporting accepted
instead for firms too small to afford it [R03 p. 533].

Coding followed the six core elements of a sufficiency-based business model — purpose,
networks, governance, ownership, finance, impact — adapted to the regenerative context
[R03 p. 533, adapting Bocken et al. 2022]. Open coding on regeneration themes and initiatives
was followed by axial coding in Atlas.ti, producing the six archetypes recorded on
[regenerative business model](../concepts/regenerative-business-model.md) [R03 p. 533].

Predominant sectors: food, consumer goods, fashion. Several cases are long-lived businesses,
some dating to the 1870s [R03 p. 529].

## Contested

The inclusion criteria verify *claims and continuity*, not *net effect*. Against
[rebound effect](../concepts/rebound-effect.md) and R02's finding on system-boundary-corrected
LCA, presence in this database is evidence of practice, not evidence of regeneration.
This reading is the wiki's, not R03's. [WG]

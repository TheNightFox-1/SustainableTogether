# Is regeneration a place-based practice, a firm-level strategy, or an efficiency property?

Raised during ingest of R03, against the definitions already carried from R01.

Three senses are in active use in the corpus, and no source reconciles them:

| Sense | Unit of analysis | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Co-evolutionary practice with the living systems of a place | a place | R01 glossary |
| A property of a firm's business model | an enterprise | R03 p. 531 |
| Recovery of exergy or product life | a device or process | R02 exec. summary |

R02 observes the third sense directly: engineering "regeneration" is overwhelmingly
efficiency-coded, while generative function relies on living systems [R02 exec. summary].
For a systems engineering working group that is the awkward one, because it is the sense our
own discipline's vocabulary already carries.

## Consequence for the compendium

[Restorative design](../concepts/restorative-design.md) is the visible symptom: R01 draws a
firm line between restoration (target state, stopping condition) and regeneration (no target
state), and R02 files ecological restoration as entry 1.1 of a regeneration taxonomy
[R02 §1.1]. Both are defensible inside their own literature.

## What would settle it

Ingesting R13 and R14 would give the WG a method for this rather than a vote — how to scope a
domain, and how to write definitions that make the distinction testable. Both are in the
registry, both currently `pending`. [WG]

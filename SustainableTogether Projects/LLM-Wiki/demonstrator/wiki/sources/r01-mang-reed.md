# R01 — Mang & Reed, *Regenerative Development and Design*

Pamela Mang and Bill Reed, Regenesis Group and Story of Place Institute.
Chapter 303, *Encyclopedia of Sustainability Science & Technology*.

## What it supplies

The corpus's definitional backbone for the design tradition. Its glossary is the source of
record for [regenerative design](../concepts/regenerative-design.md),
[regenerative development](../concepts/regenerative-development.md) and
[restorative design](../concepts/restorative-design.md), and defines the surrounding
vocabulary the WG will need: biomimicry, cradle-to-cradle, ecoliteracy, ecological
sustainability, living systems thinking, permaculture, place, locational patterns, systems
thinking [R01 glossary].

Its framing claim: green and sustainable design have focused on minimising damage and using
resources efficiently — slowing degradation — while regenerative approaches seek to reverse
the degeneration of natural systems through a whole-systems approach [R01 §II].

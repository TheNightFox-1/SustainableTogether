# R02 — Regeneration Across Earth's Systems: A Comprehensive Solution Taxonomy

WG-produced review. 144 regeneration solutions across ten domains, each entry carrying
canonical name, mechanism, scale, TRL/maturity, provenance, primary citations with DOIs,
quantified impact, and constraints [R02 exec. summary].

## What it is good for

- The only source in the corpus that spans ecological, materials, energy, atmospheric, built,
  socio-technical, Indigenous, biomimetic and commons-governance regeneration in one frame.
- Each entry pairs impact with a named critique, so contested claims arrive already contested.
- Its **gap analysis** is the most directly actionable content for this WG — see
  [MRV for living systems](../concepts/mrv-living-systems.md).
- Its **MBSE/SysML v2 addendum** triages which solutions are formally modellable, and is
  explicitly evaluative rather than part of the taxonomy [R02 addendum].

## Three headline findings

1. The most quantitatively validated regenerative solutions are ancient and Indigenous —
   farmer-managed natural regeneration in Niger (~5–6 Mha, ~200 million trees), West Arnhem
   Land cultural burning (37.7% wildfire emissions cut, >4.8M carbon credits), Sahel zaï pits
   (drought yields up to +500%), Tanzanian ngitili (~500,000 ha across 833 villages)
   [R02 exec. summary].
2. Engineering "regeneration" is overwhelmingly efficiency-coded; generative function relies
   on living systems [R02 exec. summary].
3. The literature contains substantial overclaiming, with peer-reviewed rebuttals for Savory's
   holistic grazing, the Bastin restoration potential, Rodale's >100% drawdown claim, ReFi
   token markets, biochar permanence and ocean alkalinity enhancement [R02 exec. summary].

## Handling caution

Not peer-reviewed. Every quantitative claim taken from R02 into a concept page must carry the
primary reference R02 cites, not R02 alone. Recorded in `raw/registry.md`.

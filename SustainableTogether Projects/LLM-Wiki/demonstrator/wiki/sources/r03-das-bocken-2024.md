# R03 — Das & Bocken (2024)

*Sustainable Production and Consumption* 49:529–544. Open access, CC BY.
Maastricht Sustainability Institute and IIIEE Lund.

## Argument

Regenerative business models go beyond net zero to actively restore the natural and social
systems a firm operates in [R03 p. 529]. Research on the topic is largely conceptual; the
paper's contribution is empirical — a practice review of 84 evidenced cases, and a typology
of six archetypal strategies derived from them [R03 p. 529].

It positions regeneration against circular economy explicitly: closing, narrowing and slowing
loops is harm reduction, and firms in practice favour the loop strategies that also save cost
[R03 pp. 529–530, citing Bocken et al. 2016, Allwood 2014, Ritala et al. 2018].

Concept history in the paper runs Dias (2018) on regenerative development, through Hawken
(2021) and Polman & Winston (2021) on regeneration and net positive, to Hahn & Tampe (2021)
and Konietzko et al. (2023) on regenerative business models [R03 p. 531].

## What it supplies to the wiki

The six archetypes and the method behind them —
[regenerative business model](../concepts/regenerative-business-model.md) and

## Handling caution

Case data is secondary: company web pages, public reports, newspaper articles [R03 p. 533].
The inclusion criteria verify communication, continuity and revenue — not net effect.

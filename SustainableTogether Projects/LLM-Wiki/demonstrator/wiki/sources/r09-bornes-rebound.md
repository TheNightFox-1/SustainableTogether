# R09 — Bornes (2025), INCOSE SWS Ep. 01

Laetitia Bornes, from a PhD in computer science at ENAC & ISAE-SUPAERO on socio-digital
sustainability [R09 p. 1]. Presented in the INCOSE Sustainability Webinar Series, June 2025.

## Argument

Techno-solutionist approaches fail because the improvement and its consequences sit at
different system levels: optimising car engines while total fuel consumption rises; battery
production displacing impact into mining [R09 p. 4]. Sustainability and unsustainability are
properties of a system as a whole [R09 p. 5, quoting Knowles et al. 2018], and sustainable
HCI/ICT/SE "has often fallen short on practical advice and on suitable techniques that are
concrete enough to be actionable" [R09 p. 5, quoting Raghavan & Pargman 2017].

The research question follows: can designers take a practical approach to a problem this
large without being reductive?

## The worked case

A second-hand resale platform, its commissioned avoided-emissions figure, and what a
consequential rather than attributional frame does to it [R09 pp. 6–9]. Detail on
[rebound effect](../concepts/rebound-effect.md).

## Why it is in the pilot corpus

It is the corpus's clearest demonstration that a defensible number can be useless for design
decisions — the same failure mode that makes a regenerative claim unfalsifiable.

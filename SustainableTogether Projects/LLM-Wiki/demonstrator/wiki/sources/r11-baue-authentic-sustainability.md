# R11 — Baue / r3.0 (2025), INCOSE SWS Ep. 04

Bill Baue, Senior Director at r3.0, INCOSE Sustainability Webinar Series, 29 October 2025.

## Argument

Sustainability standards abandoned the one principle that made them about sustainability.
R11 calls the period 2002–2013 "the lost decade" and walks the documentary trail: GRI G2
(2002) introduces Sustainability Context as limits and demands; G3 (2006) adds materiality
alongside it and introduces the materiality matrix; G3.1 (2011) displaces context; G4 (2013)
adds no further guidance despite 66 experts petitioning for it; SASB's 2013 conceptual
framework rejects thresholds; the IIRC framework retains carrying capacity of the capitals
[R11 slides 4–25].

The positive claim is a measurement one: sustainability measurement needs numeration *and*
denomination — a quantity and the threshold it is compared to [R11 slides 20–21].

The lineage of the threshold idea is traced from Plato's *Laws* and the Plimsoll line through
Meadows, *Limits to Growth*, Catton's phantom carrying capacity, and on to planetary
boundaries and the doughnut [R11 slides 7–17].

## Why it is in the pilot corpus

It is the strongest counter-position in the corpus to the three-pillar definition of
sustainability that an INCOSE audience arrives with, and it is the source that forced
[Q01](../questions/q01-which-sustainability.md) open.

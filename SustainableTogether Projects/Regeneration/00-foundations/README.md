## Key concept for the Task-Force

The regenerative-dynamics ontology in `01-theory-and-ontology/` (built from Fischer et al., 2024) draws a distinction the Task-Force should hold onto: restoration, remediation and repair are exogenous fixes — humans acting on nature. They are distinct from endogenous regeneration, and they are not themselves regenerative unless the resulting system becomes self-sustaining. They can, however, be prerequisites for it.

This matters for what Group 2 designs: the system must not just restore, it must initiate endogenous regenerative dynamics that continue with ongoing input.

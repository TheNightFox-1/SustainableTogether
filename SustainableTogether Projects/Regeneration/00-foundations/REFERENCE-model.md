# Reference Model — Regeneration in PV (Current State)

**Version:** 0.1 (2026-07-04)
**Status:** Initial (to be refined through DS-I)

## What This Represents

The Reference Model is a graphical representation of the **current reality** — how things are today. It shows the factors, their relationships, and the categories they belong to.

For our research, the Reference Model captures the conventional PV industry state (SolarX) — a linear, product-sales model with limited ecological and social integration.

## Factors

### Natural Capital
- **SOIL:** Soil health at PV site (baseline: conventional PV land use)
- **BIO:** On-site biodiversity (baseline: PV as mono-use)
- **WATER:** Water management (baseline: standard drainage, no retention enhancement)
- **GHG:** Lifecycle GHG intensity (baseline: ~20-30 gCO2eq/kWh for current PV)

### Social Capital
- **COMMUNITY:** Community engagement (baseline: minimal, transactional)
- **WEALTH:** Local wealth retention (baseline: most value extracted by external owners)
- **ACCESS:** Energy access for local community (baseline: standard market pricing)

### Financial Capital
- **REVENUE:** Revenue streams (baseline: electricity sales only)
- **COSTS:** Capital and operating costs (baseline: standard PV model)
- **INVESTOR:** Investor returns (baseline: conventional IRR targets)

### Manufactured Capital
- **PRODUCT:** PV system design (baseline: conventional module + racking)
- **CIRCULARITY:** Material recovery at EOL (baseline: low, ~50-70% mass recovery)
- **SUPPLY-CHAIN:** Supply chain (baseline: conventional, high carbon intensity)

## Links (Current Degenerative Dynamics)

1. **Linear material flow:** raw materials → product → waste (no circularity)
2. **Externalised costs:** ecological/social costs not internalised in financial model
3. **Short-term focus:** 20-25yr project life, minimal post-EOL planning
4. **Siloed optimisation:** financial, ecological, social optimised separately (if at all)
5. **Single revenue stream:** electricity sales only, no value from ecological/social outcomes

## Categories

```
CONVENTIONAL PV SYSTEM
├── Natural (degenerative)
│   ├── Soil degradation (land sealed)
│   ├── Biodiversity loss (mono-use)
│   ├── Water management (standard)
│   └── GHG emissions (moderate)
├── Social (minimal)
│   ├── Community engagement (transactional)
│   ├── Wealth extraction (external)
│   └── Energy access (market-priced)
├── Financial (viable but narrow)
│   ├── Revenue: electricity sales only
│   ├── Costs: standard CAPEX/OPEX
│   └── Returns: conventional IRR
└── Manufactured (linear)
    ├── Conventional product
    ├── Low circularity
    └── High-carbon supply chain
```

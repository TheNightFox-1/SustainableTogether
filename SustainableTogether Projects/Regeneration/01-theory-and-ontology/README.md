# Theory and Ontology

Academic and theoretical grounding for the Task-Force. This folder holds the WG-facing ontology artifact and the general regeneration literature. The Schaltegger corpus and the Fischer anchor paper live in Hamza's doctoral research folder — see `../PhD/`.

## The anchor paper

**Fischer, Farny, Abson, Zuin Zeidler, von Salisch, Schaltegger, Martín-López, Temperton & Kümmerer (2024).** "Mainstreaming regenerative dynamics for sustainability." *Nature Sustainability* 7, 964–972. → PDF in `../PhD/Stefan Schaltegger/`.

Open `regenerative-dynamics-ontology.html` in a browser for an interactive version of the ontology. Key concepts:
- **Regenerative dynamics** — the upward helix: desired outcomes improve repeatedly, driven by endogenous momentum and sustained by ongoing practice and resource input
- **Regenerative system** — draws on outside energy, raises a desired outcome until natural limits, without negative spill-overs
- **Restoration vs regeneration** — restoration is exogenous; regeneration becomes self-sustaining
- **Degenerative dynamics** — the mirror: a downward helix that many sustainability problems consist of

## Schaltegger corpus → `../PhD/Stefan Schaltegger/`

Schaltegger (Leuphana University Lüneburg) is one of the leading researchers on business models for sustainability. His papers were part of Hamza's doctoral reading and live in the PhD folder. They cover: sustainable entrepreneurship (2011), business models for sustainability (2015), stakeholder theory and business models, managing organizations in the natural environment (2025), sustainability accounting, the Sustainability Balanced Scorecard (Figge 2002), services for the sustainability transformation (German), and rebound-to-reinforcement effects.

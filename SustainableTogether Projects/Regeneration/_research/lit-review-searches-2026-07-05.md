## Cross-Cutting Gaps Identified

1. **No paper** explicitly addresses "regenerative PV business model" as a coherent concept
2. **No paper** links agrivoltaic biodiversity gains to financial viability of regenerative PaaS
3. **No paper** integrates MBSE + SD + LCA as a unified design methodology (except our own prior work)
4. Very few papers address **community wealth retention** as a quantifiable BM variable in energy systems
5. The regenerative dynamics concept (Fischer 2024) is **not yet applied** to energy/engineered systems

These gaps = our contribution space.

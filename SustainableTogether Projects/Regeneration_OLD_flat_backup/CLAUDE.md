# CLAUDE.md — Project Context: Regenerative PV Framework

## Project identity
**Goal:** Develop a generic, lifecycle-complete, scale-agnostic, engineer-facing **decision-support methodology for designing regenerative PV systems** — systems that not only reduce environmental impact but actively generate net-positive ecological, social, and economic outcomes.

**Owner:** Hamza Bassam, trainer/coach/consultant at oose eG, Hamburg. Works at the intersection of MBSE, SysML v2, and sustainability. Runs the SysML Club (online learning platform). Has an active semantic integration prototype: openLCA ↔ SysML v2 via Python → rdflib → GraphDB / OWL (Stages 1–3).

## Style rules (enforce in every response)
- SysML v2 **textual notation only** — never v1 syntax, never graphical
- Citations must be real and verifiable (DOIs preferred); never invent references
- Copyright: ≤15 words per quote, one quote per source
- MBSE is the **final formalization layer only** — never front-end framing

- Apply ReFi / holistic-grazing / Bastin / Rodale critiques — flag overclaiming wherever it appears

- Breadth before depth; generic before MBSE-specific
- Language: English (technical content), German where Hamza switches

### Deliverable 1 — Regeneration solution taxonomy (COMPLETE)
File: `compass_artifact_wf-bd4bf24c-591b-487c-a928-59ce5c04db03_text_markdown.md`

**144 distinct regeneration solutions** across 10 domains, with full citations (DOIs), TRL/maturity ratings, quantified impacts, and critical rebuttals.

Domains:
1. Biological / ecological regeneration
2. Material / circular-economy regeneration
3. Energy regeneration and recovery
4. Built environment and infrastructure
5. Water systems regeneration
6. Atmosphere and carbon
7. Socio-technical and systemic frameworks
8. Indigenous and traditional regenerative practices (first-class)
9. Bio-inspired / biomimetic technologies
10. Social and cultural regeneration

**Five recurring cross-cutting mechanisms** (anchor for all design work):
- Loop closure (technical and biological cycles)
- Hydrological retention and slow release
- Photosynthetic carbon transfer to recalcitrant pools
- Biological self-organization under managed disturbance
- Co-evolutionary place-based design

**Seven binding constraint gaps:** MRV, coupled hydro-bio-geochemical modelling, LCA rigor for "regenerative" claims, climate-trajectory feedback, Indigenous data sovereignty, equity/gentrification, governance pathways.

### Deliverable 2 — Methodology architecture (APPROVED IN CONCEPT, awaiting execution)

Three macro-phases, eight steps:

**A · Set context**
1. Frame ambition — Triple Top Line (C2C: Economy + Ecology + Equity, all positive)
2. Read the place — Regenesis *Story of Place* (bioregion, watershed, biota, climate signature, cultural history, Indigenous knowledge)
3. Diagnose state — local Doughnut Economics instance + ISO 14040/14044 baseline LCA of conventional PV at this site

**B · Design**
4. Map lifecycle — 8 PV lifecycle stages × 6 forms of capital → degeneration/regeneration matrix
5. Pick solutions — populate matrix from 144-solution catalogue using five recurring mechanisms
6. Synthesize — formalize in SysML v2 textual notation with openLCA hooks

**C · Realize and learn**
7. Business model — Stahel Performance Economy, EMF circular archetypes, EU Taxonomy/CSRD alignment, ReFi caution
8. Implement — citizen-science-grade MRV, adaptive management, living-lab posture, feedback to phase 1

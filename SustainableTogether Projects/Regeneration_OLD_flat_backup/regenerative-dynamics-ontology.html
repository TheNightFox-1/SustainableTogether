regenerative dynamics

Desired outcomes regenerate not once but over and over — an “upward helix”. Partly self-perpetuating and endogenous, yet shaped by context and needing ongoing energy, labour or materials to be sustained. This is the central concept the whole framework is built around. Click any node to explore.

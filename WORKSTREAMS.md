## What are WG Task Groups?

**WG Task Groups** are **ongoing workstreams** that contribute to SustainableTogether's 3-milestone roadmap:
1. **SolarX AS-IS Complete** (Milestone 1) — capture current state
2. **SustainaSun v1** (Milestone 2) — transform to sustainable model
3. **DPP Integration** (Milestone 3) — close the loop via Digital Product Passport

Unlike **GitHub Issues** (concrete, 1–2 week deliverables), WG Task Groups are:
- **Ongoing research & community activities** (months to years)
- **Led by one or more experts** (lead + optional co-lead)
- **Open to volunteers** who want deeper engagement
- **Coordinated at monthly WG meetings**
- **May spawn multiple GitHub issues** as work progresses

## How to Get Involved

### **If You See a Workstream You're Passionate About:**

1. **Read the WG Leadership Tracker** — [CSV file](SustainableTogether%20Approach/WG_Leadership_Tracker_csv.csv)
2. **Check the status:**
   - 🟩 **Active** — role already filled; can still join as a participant
   - 🟠 **Open** — no lead assigned; we need you!
3. **Contact the lead** (if active) or **volunteer in the tracker comments**
4. **Attend monthly INCOSE Sustainability WG meetings** to sync with peers
5. **Coordinate deliverables** as we hit each milestone

### **If You're Not Sure Where You Fit:**

- **Email:** sustainability@incose.net
- **INCOSE Connect:** Post in the WG's community space
- **This repo:** Open a [Discussion](../../discussions) describing your interests & expertise

## Leadership Expectations

All WG task group leads and participants are expected to:

1. **Reliability** — Follow through on commitments; communicate early if circumstances change
2. **Sufficient capacity** — Only volunteer if you can dedicate estimated 1–2 hours/week
3. **Ownership & initiative** — Take responsibility; proactively drive your area forward
4. **Regular reporting** — Provide a brief progress update at every monthly WG meeting

## Strategic Alignment

Each task group contributes to the **Generic Approach Framework** — an 8-layer sustainability transformation journey:

```
Layer 1: Strategic & Conceptual (Standards, Mission & Vision, Design for Behavior)
Layer 2: Product & Industrial (Business Model, Enterprise Architecture, Product, Enabling Systems)
Layer 3: Industrialization & Production (Manufacturing, Supply Chain, Lifecycle Operations)
Layer 4: Operation / Service (Product Use, R-Strategies, Biological Cycles)
Layer 5: Retirement (Eliminate Waste)

Holistic Topics:
- Systems Thinking & System Dynamics
- Digital Engineering (MBSE, digital twins, simulation)
- Life Cycle Assessment (environmental quantification)
- Risk Analysis (environmental, social, regulatory, supply chain)
```

For detailed framework explanation, see [SustainableTogether Approach / CLAUDE.md](SustainableTogether%20Approach/CLAUDE.md).

# Onboarding Wall

Welcome! This is the **onboarding wall** — a living list of everyone who has joined SustainableTogether.

Adding yourself here is your **first contribution exercise**. It is deliberately small and safe: by adding one line about yourself, you practice the complete GitHub loop — raise an issue, edit a file, commit, and open a pull request. Once your PR is merged, your name appears below and you are officially on board.

Full step-by-step instructions are in the **[GitHub Onboarding guide](github-onboarding.md#your-first-contribution-add-yourself-to-the-wall)**.

---

## How to add yourself

1. **Raise an issue** using the **"I'm on board 👋"** template on the [Issues tab](https://github.com/TheNightFox-1/SustainableTogether/issues/new/choose).
2. **Edit this file** and add one row to the table below, just above the `<!-- add your row above this line -->` marker.
3. **Commit** your change with a clear message, e.g. `Add: <your name> to onboarding wall`.
4. **Open a pull request** and link your issue with `Closes #N`.

Keep the format exactly as shown. Fill in only what you are comfortable sharing publicly.

```
| Your Name | Your role or background | Country | What brings you here |
```

# Presentations

## AI Agents for MBSE — Solar Panel (2025)

**File:** `AI Agents for MBSE - 2025-05-22 - Solar Panel.pdf`

A presentation exploring the application of AI agents to Model-Based Systems Engineering in the context of solar panel systems. Covers how AI can assist with system design, model generation, and analysis within the SustainaSun framework.

**Date:** May 22, 2025

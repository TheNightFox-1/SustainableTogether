# Sustainability Webinar Series

The INCOSE Sustainability Webinar Series brings together experts in systems engineering, sustainable business design, and sustainability standards. Slide decks are located in the [`SustainabilityWebinarSeries/`](https://github.com/TheNightFox-1/SustainableTogether/tree/main/SustainabilityWebinarSeries) folder.

---

## Systematically Designing Businesses Fit-for-the-Future (2025)

**File:** `20250709 Systematically Designing Businesses Fit-for-the-Future for INCOSE v1.04s.pdf`

A webinar on applying systems thinking and design methods to build businesses that are genuinely fit for a sustainable future. Covers frameworks and practical approaches for business transformation.

**Date:** July 9, 2025

---

## Hacking Sustainability Standards to Achieve Authentic Sustainability

**File:** `INCOSE Hacking Sustainability Standards to Achieve Authentic Sustainability (1).pdf`

A provocative and practical look at how to interpret and apply INCOSE sustainability standards in ways that achieve real, authentic sustainability outcomes rather than compliance theater.
